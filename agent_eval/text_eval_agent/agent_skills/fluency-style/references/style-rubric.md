# Fluency Style Rubric

## Minor

Use Minor when the target text is understandable and only slightly stiff, lightly unnatural, mildly translationese, or imperfect in word choice.

A single local collocation, literal phrase, or non-native word choice is Minor when the intended meaning remains immediately clear and the surrounding sentence is structurally usable.

## Major

Use Major when grammar, word order, collocation, register, or wording clearly harms readability and requires the reader to infer the intended meaning.

### Major readability gate

Confirm Major only when the reader must reconstruct the sentence structure, participant roles, action, or logical relation, or when multiple interacting defects prevent a direct reading. Conspicuous awkwardness alone is not enough.

## Critical

Use Critical when the target text is unreadable, heavily garbled, structurally broken, or unusable for understanding important instructions, warnings, public guidance, safety text, legal text, medical text, or permission text.

## Boundary

If the text is fluent but semantically wrong, do not report a Fluency error. Leave it to Faithfulness.
